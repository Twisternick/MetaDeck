MetaDeck

A free online card battler where every deck is a video game genre. Play in your browser: no download, no install.

Ten genre decks

  • FPS: stealthy openers, Suppression, and Headshot finishers on damaged targets.
  • Horror: Fear weakens attackers, Haunt punishes removal, and the dead keep coming back.
  • Platformer: hop over Guards with Double Jump and shrug off hits with Power-Ups and Checkpoints.
  • RPG: heroes earn XP in combat and Level Up; gear and spells make them legends.
  • Racing: bank Nitro, then spend it on Boost effects and Slipstream-powered rushers.
  • Fighting: chain cheap cards into big Combo turns; Counter fighters punish attackers.
  • Sports: go wide, keep Momentum rolling, and turn it around in the Clutch.
  • Strategy: ramp your Bandwidth behind Fortified walls, then let Structures grind the enemy down.
  • City Builder: populate the board with Citizens, Tax enemy spells, and reward Districts.
  • Card Game: rewrite cards with Errata, change the rules with House Rules, and draw piles of cards.

How you play

Learn the basics in the tutorial and play the AI, then take your deck online for ranked seasons and matches with friends. Share your deck lists as deck codes.
