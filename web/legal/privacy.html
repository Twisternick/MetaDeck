Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

The short version. You can play MetaDeck as a guest without giving us anything personal: guest progress stays in your own browser. If you make an account, we keep your account name, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password, your birth year and your game progress, so it follows you to any device. We use no advertising, no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics, and we never sell your data. You can download a copy of your data or delete your account at any time from the Account screen.

What we collect, and why

If you make an account

WhatWhy

We process account data because it’s needed to provide the account you asked for (contract), and gameplay events and security data because we have a legitimate interest in running a fair, working game.

Everyone, including guests

  • Your IP address is used while you’re connected to limit abuse (too many sign-ups, sign-in attempts or messages from one address). It isn’t stored with your account or in our analytics.
  • Anonymous visit counts. To count visitors, we combine your IP address and browser type with a random value that changes every day and is never stored, and keep only the scrambled result. This lets us count visitors per day but not recognise you from one day to the next or work out who you are.
  • Error reports. If the game hits an error in your browser, it sends us the error message and where in the code it happened, so we can fix it. No personal data is included.
  • Online matches go through our server, which sees the moves you make and the name you play under.

Cookies and storage on your device

WhatWhy
md_session cookieKeeps you signed in (only if you sign in). Strictly necessary; expires after 30 days.
Browser storageGuest progress, your deck lists, preferences, sound volume and which tips you’ve seen. It stays on your device; clearing your browser data removes it.

We don’t use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, so there’s no cookie banner.

Who else sees data

  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) sells Gems to you as our reseller and merchant of record. When you buy, you give your email, payment details and address to Paddle directly, under Paddle’s privacy policy. Paddle tells us the order number, what was bought, the amount and your country.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website and protects it from attacks, so it handles your IP address and requests. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Other players see your account name (or guest name) and your moves in online matches.

The game loads nothing from other companies’ servers: its code, art, sounds and fonts all come from our own. The only exception is the checkout page, which loads Paddle’s checkout when you buy Gems. We don’t sell or rent personal data, and we don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, progress, sessions and gameplay events at once; matches you played stay in the history without any link to you.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, even after you delete your account, but without your account name.
  • Backups of the database are kept for 14 days, so deleted data disappears from them within 14 days.
  • Server logs (including error reports) are kept for 30 days.
  • Anonymous visit counts are kept as long as they’re useful.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Get a copy of your data: Account → Your data → Download my data.
  • Delete your account and its data: Account → Delete account.
  • Correct data, object to or restrict how we use it, or ask us anything about it: email [CONTACT EMAIL].

If you’re in the EU, the UK or another place with data protection law, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

MetaDeck accounts are for players 13 and older, and sign-up asks for your birth year. Younger players can play as guests, which doesn’t collect personal data beyond the anonymous counts above. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as salted hashes, sessions only as hashes, the site is served over HTTPS, and the server rate-limits sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top, and tell signed-in players in the game about important changes.
