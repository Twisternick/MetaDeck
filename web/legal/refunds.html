Refund Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

The short version. If you change your mind, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying and we’ll give it as long as you haven’t spent any of that purchase’s Gems. If something went wrong (you were charged but got no Gems, or charged twice), we’ll always put it right.

Who you bought from

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns, so refunds are paid by Paddle to the payment method you used.

Your right to cancel

Gems are digital content delivered to your account straight away. When you buy them, you ask for immediate delivery and agree that you lose the legal right to cancel (the 14-day “cooling-off” period in the EU and UK) once they’re delivered. We still offer the goodwill refunds below.

When we refund

  • Unused Gems, within 14 days: a full refund of a purchase if none of its Gems have been spent.
  • Something went wrong: you were charged but the Gems never arrived, you were charged twice, or a fault in the game used up your Gems. We refund or restore them, whenever it happened.
  • Unauthorised purchases (for example, made by a child without permission): contact us and we’ll look at each case.

Other requests, such as Gems already spent on packs, cosmetics or the season track, aren’t normally refunded, except where the law says otherwise.

What a refund does to your account

A refund removes that purchase’s Gems from your account. If some were already spent, your Gem balance goes below zero and Gem purchases in the shop are paused until it’s back above zero (Gold, matches and everything else keep working). The same happens if a payment is reversed through your bank (a chargeback).

How to ask

Reply to the receipt email from Paddle, or use paddle.net to find your order, or contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with your account name and the order number. We answer within a few days.
